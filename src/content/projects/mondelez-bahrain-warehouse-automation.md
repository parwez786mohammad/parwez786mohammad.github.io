businessProblem: >
  Mondelez's Bahrain distribution center relied on manual pallet handling that limited storage density,
  slowed order fulfillment, and introduced handling errors — constraining growth as regional volumes increased.
myRole: >
  I managed planning, coordination, and execution of the ASRS deployment end-to-end, including risk
  management, issue resolution, and performance optimization through go-live.
solution: >
  We deployed a 10-floor Automated Storage and Retrieval System with 10 shuttle robots, 2 lifts, and 4
  conveyors across 32 aisles per side, handling pallets up to 1,000kg, fully integrated with Mondelez's
  existing SAP environment.
challenges:
  - "Coordinating a live warehouse retrofit without halting ongoing distribution operations"
  - "Integrating a new ASRS platform cleanly with an established SAP WMS"
  - "Managing multiple specialist vendors (conveyors, shuttles, lifts) on one timeline"
outcomes:
  - "50% increase in storage efficiency"
  - "35% improvement in order fulfillment speed"
  - "Marked reduction in manual handling errors"
  - "Seamless integration with existing SAP systems"
keyLearnings:
  - "Technology lessons: system specification (capacity, throughput, floors) must be locked before vendor selection, not after"
  - "Risk management: phased cutover plans protect live operations during automation rollouts"
  - "Stakeholder management: warehouse operators need to be part of design, not just end-users of it"

The Mondelez Bahrain project turned a conventional pallet warehouse into a fully automated, SAP-integrated
distribution center — a project defined by tight coordination across hardware vendors, IT, and live
operations.
